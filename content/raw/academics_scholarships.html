Merit awards

Rewarding academic excellence

High performers in entrance examinations and prior academics may qualify for substantial tuition reductions. We believe talent should never be limited by cost.

  • Based on entrance exam scores and +2 results
  • Tiered awards from 25% to 75% tuition coverage
  • Renewable each semester with GPA requirements
  • Open to both BIT and B.Tech Ed IT applicants
Need-based aid

Support for deserving students

Students facing financial hardship can apply for need-based grants. Our committee reviews each case individually with confidentiality and care.

  • Income and family circumstance documentation required
  • Partial tuition grants and installment flexibility
  • Priority for students from underserved regions
  • Combined with merit awards where applicable

FAQ

Scholarship questions

Answers to the most common financial aid inquiries.

Can I apply for multiple scholarships?+

Students may be considered for more than one category, but total aid is capped per institutional policy. The committee assigns the most suitable award.

Do scholarships cover full tuition?+

Merit scholarships can cover up to 75% of tuition. Partial awards and need-based grants are also available depending on funds.

Is the scholarship renewable?+

Most awards require maintaining a minimum GPA each semester and good academic standing to continue receiving support.

When should I apply?+

Submit your scholarship application alongside or immediately after accepting your admission offer, before the enrollment deadline.
