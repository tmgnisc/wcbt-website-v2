Admissions

How to apply

Admissions are open for the upcoming intake. Follow these steps to begin your application at WCBT Jhapa Campus.

01

Check eligibility

Review academic requirements for +2 or equivalent qualifications in relevant streams.

02

Submit application

Complete the online form with transcripts, identification, and program preference.

03

Entrance assessment

Attend the admission test and interview scheduled by the admissions office.

04

Enrollment

Confirm your seat, complete fee payment, and join orientation week.
