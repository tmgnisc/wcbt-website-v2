Getting here

Easy to reach in Jhapa

WCBT is located on the premises of Nidi Secondary School & Indreni Campus in Jhapa, Nepal, with convenient access by public and private transport.

  • Central location in Jhapa, Eastern Nepal
  • Accessible by bus and local transport
  • Parking available for visitors
  • Assistance available — just call ahead
